COMPONENT ROLES & SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

1. INGESTION & CHUNKING (src/ingestion/)
   - StructureAwareChunker: Splits documents along natural semantic boundaries
     (sections, headings, sentences) rather than naive character slicing.
   - Metadata Propagation: Ensures title, section hierarchy, chunkIndex, and
     timestamps are bundled with each chunk.

2. RETRIEVAL & HYBRID SEARCH (src/retrieval/)
   - DenseRetriever: Captures semantic intent and latent concepts.
   - BM25Retriever: Captures exact term frequency, keyword precision, IDs, codes.
   - ScoreFusion: Combines disjoint score distributions using:
     * Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF): rank-based, resilient to score scale variance.
     * Normalized Score Fusion: min-max normalized weighted linear interpolation.
   - MetadataFilter: Pre-filters candidate chunks before or during retrieval.

3. TWO-STAGE RE-RANKING (src/retrieval/)
   - Stage 1 (Wide Recall): High-recall hybrid retrieval fetches top-M (e.g. 20-50).
   - Stage 2 (High Precision): Cross-encoder evaluates query-chunk interactions
     jointly with token-level cross-attention to produce top-K (e.g. 3-5).

4. QUERY TRANSFORMATION (src/transformation/)
   - QueryRewriter: Clarifies ambiguous queries, resolves pronouns/coreferences.
   - QueryDecomposer: Breaks complex multi-hop queries into atomic sub-queries.
   - HyDE: Generates a hypothetical ideal answer document to retrieve by embedding.

5. RAG HARDENING (src/hardening/)
   - LostInTheMiddle: Reorders ranked chunks so the most relevant chunks are at the
     head and tail of the context prompt, where LLMs pay maximal attention.
   - ContextBudgetGuard: Truncates and packs context strictly within token budgets.
   - FreshnessManager: Validates document timestamps, applies decay multipliers,
     and invalidates stale cache entries.

6. EVALUATION SUITE (src/eval/)
   - Retrieval Eval: Measures Recall@K, MRR, nDCG, and Context Precision on labeled datasets.
   - Generation Eval: Measures Faithfulness (hallucination detector) and Answer Relevance.
   - Benchmark: Compares Naive Top-K vs Hybrid vs Two-Stage Re-ranked pipelines.
